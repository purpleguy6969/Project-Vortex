What is Project Vortex?

Project Vortex is a high-energy hackathon designed to challenge students from schools across the region to build websites that address genuine, pressing real-world problems.

Teams collaborate, prototype, and present functional websites — pushing the limits of what's possible when creative thinking meets technical execution.

This isn't about templates. It's about building something that matters.

// Rules

Competition Rules

All participants are expected to compete with integrity and follow these guidelines.

RULE 01
Eligible Participants

Participants must be currently enrolled students at a registered participating school. Each team must have a faculty supervisor.

RULE 02
Original Work Only

All code, design, and content must be created during the competition window. Pre-existing projects are not permitted.

RULE 03
Team Composition

Teams must consist of students from the same school. Cross-school collaboration is not permitted in the main competition track.

RULE 04
Submission Format

Each team must submit a live, functional website along with a brief presentation explaining the problem it solves and the approach taken.

RULE 05
Content Standards

All submitted websites must be appropriate for school audiences. Projects with offensive or inappropriate content will be disqualified.

RULE 06
Fair Play

Use of AI tools must be declared. Judges will assess the depth of student contribution. Academic integrity is non-negotiable.
